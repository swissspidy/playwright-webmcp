- **sheet-analyze**: Spreadsheet seeded with 240 rows of sales (read in pages). Answer needs every Q2 row. Prompt: "Sheet1 holds a year of sales, one row per month, region and product. Which region had the highest revenue (units times unit price) from April to June 2026, and how much was it? Reply with the region and the amount."
- **sheet-summarize**: Same seed. Write a sorted per-region summary to a new sheet. Prompt: "Sheet1 holds a year of sales, one row per month, region and product. Add a sheet named Summary with the headers Region and Revenue in row 1, then one row per region with its total revenue for the year (units times unit price), sorted from highest to lowest revenue."
- **sheet-band**: Same seed. Label all 240 rows by revenue in a new column. Prompt: "Sheet1 holds a year of sales, one row per month, region and product. In column F, put the header Band in F1, then for every data row write High if its revenue (units times unit price) is above 1000, and Normal otherwise."
- **pixel-count**: Canvas seeded with overlapping shapes. Count one colour over 64×64 pixels. Prompt: "How many pixels on the canvas are the colour #bf2a7a? Reply with just the number."
- **pixel-mirror**: Left half seeded. Mirror it onto the right half: read, transform, write. Prompt: "Make the picture mirror-symmetric: copy the left half of the canvas (columns 0 to 31) mirrored onto the right half (columns 32 to 63), so that column 63 matches column 0, column 62 matches column 1, and so on. Keep the left half as it is."
- **shop-gifts**: Sport shop: about 8 KB of search results to filter and sum. Prompt: "What would it cost to buy one of every child-size product tagged gift that has at least 5 in stock? Reply with the total."
- **shop-cart**: Sport shop: add every product that matches price and stock to the cart. Prompt: "Add one of every product that costs less than $60 and has at least 10 in stock to the cart."
